Two kinds of material, used differently.

**Concept pages** build the model. Each opens with a failure you can reproduce,
explains why the mechanism was designed that way, and ends with a lab you run and
self-check questions you can't answer from memory alone. Start here.

**Reference questions** test recall once the model is built. Question, model
answer, why it matters, follow-ups. Use them to self-test, not to learn from
cold.

## Working with it

- **Search covers collapsed answers.** `Ctrl` `K` searches the full text of every
  answer and follow-up, not just the visible headings.
- **Every question is a deep link.** `/docs/java/concurrency#q48` opens that one
  answer expanded and scrolls to it — useful for citing a specific claim.
- **Numbering restarts per bank**, so `#q1` exists three times. The path is what
  disambiguates: `/docs/java/core-language#q1` and
  `/docs/data/modelling-indexing#q1` are different questions.
- **46 diagrams** sit inside the pages they illustrate — 19 in the reference
  answers (the JVM memory areas, G1's mixed collection, MVCC tuple versions,
  Kafka's high watermark, the transactional outbox, hexagonal ports and
  adapters, and others), 26 more built into the concept pages, each showing
  something the reference bank doesn't already, and one on the study path.
- **Every concept page ends with its own study path** — the full chain to read
  before it, and what it opens up. The whole graph is on
  [The study path](/docs/concepts).

## What's here, and what isn't

The reference conversion is **complete**: all 30 sections, 419 questions, 295
follow-ups, converted from `_source/` without rewriting a word of the answers.

The concept pages are the point of the library, and both planned tiers are
**complete**: all 13 foundational pages and all 21 Core pages, 34 in total, each
explaining three to six reference questions in depth — most often five — rather
than restating them. 154 of the 419 reference questions are claimed by a concept
page, and that number is supposed to be low: a small number of deeply understood
mechanisms generates correct answers to a large number of questions.

What is left is deliberate. The `leading/` track is scaffolding only — ten
topics, each with six questions and no prose. Those essays have to come from
real experience rather than be generated, so the questions are the page until
they are answered. The Specialist tier is recorded in `CLAUDE.md` as a list of
names rather than in page frontmatter — none of it is a commitment, and the
study path links only to pages that exist.
