What this essay should cover: how you form a view of where the system needs to
be in two years, how you fund that work in quarters measured on something else,
and how you tell a strategy from a wish list.

## Questions to answer

1. What is the two-year view for a system you own or have owned? State it as
   something that could turn out to be wrong.
2. What evidence made you believe it — incident patterns, change failure rate,
   the cost of a specific class of feature, hiring? Be concrete.
3. Describe a piece of long-horizon work you got funded. What was the argument
   that worked, and who was it aimed at?
4. Describe one you failed to get funded. Was the argument wrong, the audience
   wrong, or the timing?
5. How do you sequence strategic work against delivery so the strategy survives
   a bad quarter? Give a real sequence you used.
6. What in your last strategy turned out to be wrong, and how long did it take
   you to admit it?
