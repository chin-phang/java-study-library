---
title: Saying No With Reasons That Survive Scrutiny
description: Refusing work or a design in a way that holds up when challenged by someone with more authority.
tags: [leading, decisions, communication]
---

What this essay should cover: how you refuse something — a feature, a design, a
date, a tool — with a reason specific enough to be argued with, and what you do
when you are overruled anyway.

## Questions to answer

1. Describe a no that stuck. What was the reason you gave, and why was it hard
   to dismiss?
2. Describe a no that did not stick. Was your reasoning weak, or was it right
   and unheard?
3. Where do you draw the line between a technical objection and a preference?
   Give a case where you had to admit it was the latter.
4. Describe being overruled on something you still believe you were right about.
   What did you do next, and did you record the disagreement anywhere?
5. How do you say no to someone more senior differently from someone on your own
   team? Should it be different?
6. Name a no you regret. What would the yes have cost, and what did the no cost
   instead?
