title: Growing People
description: Turning your own judgement into other people's, deliberately rather than by osmosis.

What this essay should cover: what you have actually done that made a specific
engineer better — the assignments, the reviews, the conversations — and how you
tell real growth from mere activity.

## Questions to answer

1. Name an engineer who got substantially better while working with you. What
   did you do that you believe contributed, and what would have happened anyway?
2. Describe a piece of work you handed over that you could have done faster
   yourself. What did it cost, and was the trade worth it?
3. Describe a time you let someone make a mistake you could see coming. Where is
   your line between a useful mistake and an expensive one?
4. What feedback have you given that landed badly? What was wrong — the content,
   the timing, or the setting?
5. How do you tell the difference between someone who is stuck and someone who
   is struggling? What do you do differently in each case?
6. Name something you were wrong about that a more junior engineer corrected.
   How did that change how you run reviews or design discussions?
